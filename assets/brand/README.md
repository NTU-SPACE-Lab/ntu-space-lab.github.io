# SPACE Lab identity

The SPACE LAB mark turns the **A** into a spatial origin: three directed axes
meet at one red node. The horizontal lockup pairs the bold SPACE wordmark with
a substantial LAB signature across a blue coordinate divider. The refined P
uses a lifted bowl and generous counter so its vertical stem reads cleanly at
both navigation and display sizes.

## Assets

- `space-logo-direct.png` — primary website wordmark, cropped directly from the approved concept image
- `space-fold-mark.png` — folded-space aperture emblem used in the Research approach diagram
- `space-logo.svg` — editable vector master for light backgrounds
- `space-mark.svg` — standalone mark and favicon for light backgrounds
- `space-logo-reversed.svg` — horizontal wordmark for navy/dark backgrounds
- `space-mark-reversed.svg` — standalone mark for navy/dark backgrounds

## Core colours

- Spatial navy: `#080C27`
- Coordinate blue: `#1769FF`
- Origin red: `#D71440`
- Reversed coordinate blue: `#4C91FF`
- Reversed origin red: `#F32250`

## Usage

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the diameter of its red origin node.
Use the full wordmark at widths of 165 px or larger. Below that size, use the
standalone mark. Do not recolour individual axes, add effects, or place the
light-background artwork on busy imagery.
